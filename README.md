# ProofLens

## Verifiable AI Decision Passport

**Don't just trust the AI decision. Verify how it was made.**

ProofLens turns important AI decisions into a human-readable Decision Passport.

Instead of showing only an AI answer, ProofLens also shows:

- what the AI saw
- what claims were detected
- what evidence supports those claims
- what risks remain
- how confident the AI is
- whether the decision can be verified

The goal is simple:

**AI decisions should come with evidence, not just answers.**
